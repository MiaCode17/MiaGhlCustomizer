## Product in one sentence
A white-label customizer for GoHighLevel agencies: make the portal look and feel like your own brand, without writing code.

## Audience
GoHighLevel agency owners and their small teams who resell a CRM to local businesses. They want their clients to see their logo, colors and login page, not GoHighLevel's. They are busy and practical, not developers.

## What we help with (content pillars)
- Branding: themes, logos, login page designs, loaders
- Client experience: banners, hero banners, menus, buttons, launchpad, support chat
- Onboarding: guided tours, feedback
- Time saved: changes in minutes, applied across every client portal

## Tone
Friendly, direct, no hype. Short sentences. Show the result before explaining how. Talk to one agency owner ("you"), not a crowd.

## Words/phrases to use
your brand, your clients, in minutes, no code, white-label, looks like yours

## Words/phrases to avoid
revolutionary, game-changer, supercharge, unlock, "in today's fast-paced world", too many emojis

## Platforms and formats
- Instagram/Reels/Stories: 15-20 second before/after of a portal change
- LinkedIn: short posts about agency branding wins
- X: one-tip posts and short feature announcements
